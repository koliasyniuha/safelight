Safelight

Send photos and videos to a phone on the same Wi-Fi. No account, no cloud, no cables.

How it works

  1. Open Safelight on both phones. Connect both phones to the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. On the sending phone, tap Send photos and choose the files.
  3. Choose the other phone in the list. If it does not show, scan its QR code.
  4. Accept the transfer on the receiving phone. The files go to its gallery.

Common problems

The other phone is not in the list. Make sure that both phones use the same Wi-Fi network and that Safelight is open on both. On iPhone, allow “Local Network” access in Settings › Safelight. Some guest or public networks block traffic between devices. Use the QR code or a home network.

The app cannot see my photos. Allow photo access in the phone settings for Safelight.

Restore Pro on a new phone. Open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account that you used to buy Pro.

Cancel a subscription. On iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play › Profile › Payments and subscriptions › Subscriptions.
