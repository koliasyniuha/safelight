Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

Safelight (“the app”) sends photos and videos directly from one phone to another phone on the same local network. This policy explains what data the app uses.

Summary

  • We do not have servers that receive your photos or videos.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • You do not need an account.

Photos and videos

The app reads your photo library only after you give permission, and only so that you can choose files to send. The files go directly over your local Wi-Fi network to the phone that you choose. The receiving phone saves them to its gallery after the user accepts the transfer. The files never go to us or to any other third party.

The transfer uses your local network only. The connection between the two phones is not encrypted. Use networks that you trust, such as your home Wi-Fi.

Local network and device name

To find other phones, the app announces itself on your local network with a device name and phone type (for example “iPhone”). You can change this name in the app. Other phones on the same network that run Safelight can see this name.

Camera

The app uses the camera only to scan the QR code of another phone. The app does not save or send camera images.

Data stored on your phone

The app stores your settings, your device name, and, for Pro users, a history of transfers (date, phone name, number of files, size). This data stays on your phone. Delete the app to delete it.

Purchases

Pro purchases go through the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. To check your Pro status, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase service provider. RevenueCat receives your purchase history, a random app user ID that is not connected to your name or email, and basic device data (such as the operating system version and country of the store). We use this data only to give you access to Pro.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

You can ask us to delete the purchase data that RevenueCat stores for your random user ID. Contact us through the link below. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU under the GDPR), you can also have the right to access, correct, or object to the processing of your data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
